Same Day Funding Application

Our same-day funding programs are designed for established businesses with consistent revenue. Pre-approval in 24 hours, with funds typically in your account within 3–7 days from application.

What Qualifies for Same-Day Funding

  • Business open 12+ months
  • $120,000+ in annual revenue (verifiable via bank statements)
  • 8+ monthly deposits
  • Manageable NSF activity
  • U.S.-based business

What to Have Ready

  • Last 6 months of business bank statements (full PDFs), plus month-to-date transactions.
  • Voided business check or bank letter.
  • Driver's license, front and back.
  • EIN letter.
  • If monthly revenue is $35,000+: last 90 days of credit card processing statements and an accounts receivable summary.
